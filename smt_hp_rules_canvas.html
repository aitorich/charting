SMT Daily Quarters — High-Probability Filters (4HR)

Visual reference for the four confluence rules layered on top of the SMT Daily Quarters indicator. Apply on a 4HR chart. NQ/YM open 18:00 ET · FX/Metals open 17:00 ET.

Concept — All four rules in one daily transition

HP★

Prev day (pv1..pv6, net BULL) → new day. Rule-2 line dropped from pv5 (last red). Rule-3 active window over Q1..Q4. Rule-1 swing low forms in Q3, after the daily open. Rule-4 HP★ on Q3: within-day Bull SMT + cross-day Bull SMT + interaction with the Rule-2 line.

Rule 1 — Swing AFTER daily open

Valid

3-bar fractal pivot forms inside the new day. Q3.L < Q2.L and < Q4.L → confirmed swing low. Bar_index of Q3 > Q1 open ⇒ qualifies as today's trigger swing.

Rule 1 — Swing BEFORE daily open

Invalid

Pivot inherited from prev day's pv3. Level may still be respected, but it does NOT count as today's trigger swing — Rule-1 fails.

Rule 2 — Last opposite candle (bullish bias)

Bias support ✓

Prev day net green. Last red 4HR is pv5. Mark pv5.close as horizontal level. Q1..Q3 hold above ⇒ bullish bias confirmed within first 3 quarters.

Rule 2 — Last opposite candle (bearish bias)

Bias support ✓

Prev day net red. Last green 4HR is pv5. Mark pv5.close. Q1..Q3 hold below ⇒ bearish bias confirmed within first 3 quarters.

Rule 3 — SMT must form within Q1..Q4

Within-day Bull SMT firing on Q3 (left) is full-color and tagged. Same setup firing on Q5 (right) is muted/dashed and excluded from the HP★ filter.

Rule 4 — High-probability triple confluence

HP★

Q3 fires within-day Bull SMT (vs Q1∪Q2 low) AND cross-day Bull SMT (vs prev day's MIN low held by pv4) AND the Q3 close lands back above the Rule-2 last-opposite line. Q3 also qualifies as the Rule-1 swing low ⇒ HP★.

Validity checklist & defaults

  • Rule 1 — Swing after daily open. Trigger swing must form AFTER the latest Q1 open (18:00 ET for NQ/YM, 17:00 ET for FX/metals). Default swing definition = 3-bar fractal pivot (mid bar's H/L beats both immediate neighbors).
  • Rule 2 — Last opposite candle. At prev day's Q6 close, scan pv1..pv6 and mark the LAST 4HR whose body color is opposite to the prev day's net direction (close vs open). Mark its CLOSE as a horizontal level. Extend through Q1..Q3 of the new day; expire after Q3 closes.
  • Rule 3 — Active SMT window. Within-day and cross-day SMTs fire at full priority only when current Q ≤ 4. SMTs on Q5 / Q6 still draw, but are demoted to muted/dashed and excluded from the HP★ filter.
  • Rule 4 — HP★ trigger. A within-day or cross-day SMT (Q ≤ 4) that lands on or interacts with at least one external key level: cross-day reference (prev day's MAX H / MIN L) OR the Rule-2 last-opposite line OR PDH/PDL.
  • Disqualifier. Q5/Q6 SMTs cannot earn HP★ regardless of external-level overlap.
  • Disqualifier. No qualifying Rule-1 swing on the same side ⇒ no HP★, even if Rule-3 + Rule-4 overlap (the Rule-1 swing acts as the trigger).

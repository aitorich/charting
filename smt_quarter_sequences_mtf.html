Design Principles

Silent outside C3. Chart is blank when no setup is active.

Alive inside C3. Zone lights up, signals appear, PSP fires.

Bias filters everything. Only signals aligned with daily direction show.

Visual hierarchy. Big = macro (4HR). Small = micro (1HR). Dots = execution (SMT).

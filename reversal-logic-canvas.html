SCS Reversal Logic — Visual Reference

HTF reversals confirmed by aligned-LTF CISD (no intra-candle scanning). One active reversal per direction. New reversal that takes out a previous one's swept extreme replaces it on the chart.

1. C2 Reversal depth 1

Trigger. C2 sweeps low(C1) AND closes back above it.
Visuals. Red closure line at low(C1) on HTF chart. Equilibrium box from open(C3) → midpoint of [open(C3), low(C2)] on aligned LTF + Middle TF.

2. C3 Reversal depth 2

Trigger. C2 was a continuation of C1 (swept C1's low, closed below it). C3 then sweeps low(C1) again AND closes back above it.
Visuals. Orange closure line at low(C1). Equilibrium box uses open(C4) → midpoint of [open(C4), low(C3)].

3. CISD on Reversal Only no intra-candle

When CISD is searched. Only inside the continuation HTF candle (C3 for a C2 reversal, C4 for a C3 reversal) — and only if the corresponding HTF reversal has fired.
Anchor. Open of the OLDEST candle in the consecutive bearish run that ends at/before the lowest low of that window. CISD fires when an LTF close crosses the anchor.
No intra-candle CISD. Without an HTF reversal trigger, no CISD search runs.

4. Equilibrium Box LTF + Middle TF

Where it shows. Aligned Primary LTF AND Middle TF. Not on HTF. Not anywhere else.
HTF opening line. Vertical line at the open bar — Primary LTF only. Avoids duplication on Middle TF where the eq box already anchors at the same point.

5. Invalidation — newest valid reversal wins delete prior

Rule. Each direction (bull / bear) has at most one active reversal on the chart. The active one stores its swept extreme as the invalidation level (low(C2) for a depth-1 bullish reversal; low(C1) for a depth-2 bullish reversal). Mirror for bearish.
Invalidation. When a future HTF candle closes through that swept extreme in the original trend direction, every visual tied to that reversal is deleted: closure line, equilibrium box, anchor line, run bracket, on every chart it was rendered.
Replacement. A new C2 or C3 reversal that fires later (and confirms via CISD) becomes the new active reversal. Its visuals replace the deleted ones.
Slot model: bullActive (one slot) and bearActive (one slot). Each slot stores its drawing handles + its invalidation level + its CISD pending state. Two C2 / C3 reversals in the same direction can never coexist on the chart — the older is always swept away by the newer.

Pipeline (per HTF candle close).
  1. HTF reversal detection. C2 reversal: low(C3) < low(C2) AND close(C3) > low(C2). C3 reversal: C2 was a continuation of C1, then C3 closes back across low(C1). Mirror for bearish.
  2. Invalidation pass. If low(new HTF) < bullActive.invalidationLevel AND close(new HTF) < invalidationLevel → delete bullActive. Mirror for bearish.
  3. Arm CISD pending slot. If reversal fires AND its slot is now empty, store the swept extreme as the new invalidation level and arm a CISD watch on the aligned LTF inside the next HTF candle.
  4. CISD watch (LTF only, reversal-gated). Inside the continuation HTF candle, find lowest low / highest high → walk back through the consecutive opposite-direction run → anchor = open of OLDEST candle in run. Fire when an LTF close crosses the anchor.
  5. On CISD fire. Draw closure line (HTF), equilibrium box (LTF + Middle TF), HTF opening line (LTF only), CISD anchor + bracket (LTF only). Store all handles in the active slot.
  6. On CISD timeout. Continuation HTF candle closes without CISD → drop the pending state; nothing was drawn, nothing to remove.

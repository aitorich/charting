SMT Scenarios — Visual Reference

NQ vs YM correlated pair · ICT Smart Money Technique · all scenarios from the repo's quarter-sequence indicators

1 · SMT Divergence (core idea)

BULL SMT Lower-Low Divergence

One symbol prints a new lower low; the other fails to confirm — sellers exhausted, potential reversal up.

BEAR SMT Higher-High Divergence

One symbol prints a new higher high; the other fails to confirm — buyers exhausted, potential reversal down.

2 · C1 / C2 / C3 Fractal Closure

BULL C2 Sweep low + close back above

C2 wicks below C1's low then closes back above it. C3 (next candle/block) becomes the armed zone.

BEAR C2 Sweep high + close back below

C2 wicks above C1's high then closes back below it. C3 (next candle/block) becomes the armed zone.

3 · Quarter Sequences

Within-block: 1 HR split into 4 × 15 m

Each new quarter's high/low is checked against the running max/min of earlier quarters within the same block.

Cross-block: Q4 of prev vs Q1 of current

The 4 HR base file extends SMT detection across the block boundary so straddle-divergences are not missed.

4 · PSP & C3 Zone

PSP Pair disagrees on the same bar

Inside an active C3, NQ closes one direction and YM closes the other — sharpest SMT tell, paints the bar magenta.

C3 Silent vs alive

Outside C3 the chart stays silent. When C3 is armed the background lights up and signals appear.

5 · MTF Nested Fractal (the full stack)

Daily Bias → 4HR C2 → 1HR Quarter SMT → 1HR C2 → 15m Quarter SMT → PSP

Two C1/C2/C3 sequences nested: the 4HR macro C3 contains 1HR quarters; the 1HR micro C3 contains 15m quarters where PSP fires.

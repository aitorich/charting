SMT at Key Levels — Canvas Reference

The high-quality SMT setup: divergence happens AT a level where liquidity was resting

1 · Anatomy of a valid SMT @ key level

VALID

Level → Sweep on one symbol → Hold on the other → SMT line

NQ wicks beyond the level (liquidity grab) and closes back inside; YM never reaches the equivalent level. The asymmetric reaction at a real liquidity pool is the signal.

2 · Valid scenarios — daily liquidity (PDH / PDL)

VALID

BULL @ PDL sweep

NQ wicks below the previous-day low and closes back above it; YM holds above its PDL. Bullish reversal SMT at daily liquidity.
VALID

BEAR @ PDH sweep

NQ wicks above the previous-day high and closes back below it; YM holds below its PDH. Bearish reversal SMT at daily liquidity.

3 · Valid scenarios — other key levels

VALID

BULL @ Equal Lows (EQL)

Two prior wicks form an equal low on NQ — that's a relative-equal-low liquidity pool. Current bar takes it; YM's own EQL holds. Reversal SMT at EQL.
VALID

BEAR @ Asia Session High

Asia range = overnight liquidity. London/NY runs the Asia high on NQ; YM's Asia high holds. Bearish reversal SMT at session liquidity.

4 · Invalid patterns (look-alikes that don't qualify)

INVALID

Both sweep the level

NQ and YM both take PDL — no divergence, just a synchronized liquidity raid + continuation.
INVALID

Sweep + close through

NQ takes PDL and closes below it. That's a breakout / continuation — not a reversal SMT, even if YM holds.
INVALID

Misaligned timing

NQ swept hours ago; YM is sweeping now. Different liquidity events — no smart-money divergence.

5 · Validity checklist

For an SMT at a key level to count as a valid setup

✓
Level is structuralPDH/PDL, PWH/PWL, session high/low, swing pivot, EQH/EQL, HTF FVG, order block, weekly open.
✓
One symbol sweepsWick prints clearly beyond the level — a real liquidity grab, not just a touch.
✓
The other holdsDoes not reach beyond the equivalent level on its own chart.
✓
Same time windowBoth reactions occur on the same bar (or the same quarter for quarter-sequence SMT).
✓
Closes back insideThe sweeping bar closes back through the level — confirms rejection (C2 closure).
✓
Bias-alignedDirection matches the daily bias filter (PDH/PDL C2 from MTF v2).
✗
Both sides sweepIf both NQ and YM take the level, there is no divergence at all.
✗
Sweep + close throughA close beyond the level is a breakout / continuation, not a reversal SMT.

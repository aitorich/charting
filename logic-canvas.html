Candle Closure Logic — Reversal, Continuation, Equilibrium

Bearish setup shown (mirror for bullish). Strategy: wait for a reversal closure, then trade continuation in the new direction.

1. Reversal closure on C2 trade C3

Definition: C2 wick takes low(C1) AND close(C2) ≥ low(C1).
Action: reversal confirmed → trade C3 in the bullish direction.

2. Continuation closure on C2 no tradewait C3

Definition: C2 wick takes low(C1) AND close(C2) < low(C1).
Action: trend continues → do not trade. Roll reference to C2 and wait for C3 closure.

3. C2 continuation → C3 reversal trade C4

Sequence: C2 produces a continuation closure → ref rolls to C2. C3 then sweeps low(C2) and closes above it → reversal confirmed.
Action: trade C4 in the bullish direction.

4. Equilibrium of the reversal visual

After C2 reversal: range = open(C3) → low(C2). Equilibrium = midpoint.
Visual: shaded box from open(C3) to equilibrium. Must render on aligned LTF (1m↔15m, 3m↔30m, 5m↔1H, 15m↔4H, 30m↔1D, 1H↔1D, 4H↔1W).

Rules summary. Sweep = next candle's wick takes ref's extreme. Reversal = sweep + close back beyond ref → trade next bar. Continuation = sweep + close past ref → roll ref forward, wait. No sweep = invalid closure → keep ref, wait next bar. Equilibrium = mid of open(Cn+1) ↔ swept extreme of Cn; visual is a box from open(Cn+1) to equilibrium, mirrored on LTF.

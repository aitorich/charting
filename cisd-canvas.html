CISD — Change in State of Delivery

Reversal confirmation on the aligned LTF. Anchor = open of the last bearish (or bullish) LTF candle at/before the HTF window's extreme. CISD fires the moment a candle closes through that anchor.

1. Bullish CISD aligned LTF

Bullish CISD. Inside the HTF window, find the LTF bar with the lowest low. Walk backward to the last bearish candle at or before that low. Anchor = its open. CISD fires when an LTF bar's close rises above the anchor.

2. Bearish CISD aligned LTF

Bearish CISD. Inside the HTF window, find the bar with the highest high. Walk backward to the last bullish candle at or before that high. Anchor = its open. CISD fires when an LTF bar's close drops below the anchor.

3. Low bar is bullish — keep walking back walk back

Edge case. When the low-print bar itself is bullish, the anchor is not abandoned. Keep walking backward through any number of bars until the nearest preceding bearish candle is found, and use its open as the anchor.

4. Single or consecutive — same rule last bearish

Single vs consecutive. The rule does not care how many bearish candles precede the low. It takes the last bearish candle reachable by walking backward from the low. Open of that candle = anchor.

Definition recap. CISD on the aligned LTF confirms a reversal implied by the HTF setup. Bullish: low of HTF window → walk back to the last bearish LTF candle → anchor = its open → CISD when a candle closes above anchor. Bearish: mirror at the high.
